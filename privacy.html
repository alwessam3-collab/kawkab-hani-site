Privacy Policy

Kawkab Hani is a personal assistant application designed to help users prepare and manage content for TikTok.

The application may access TikTok account information and content permissions only after the user grants authorization through TikTok.

Kawkab Hani does not sell personal information and does not share user data with advertisers.

TikTok access tokens and related account data are used only to provide the requested features, such as preparing or uploading content as drafts for manual review.

The application does not publish content automatically without the user's review and approval.

Users may revoke TikTok access at any time through their TikTok account settings.

For privacy questions, please contact the application owner.
